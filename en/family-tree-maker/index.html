Make your family tree online

Add your brothers and sisters, aunts, uncles and cousins, type their names and, if you like, add a photo of each. The tree draws itself and you download it as a PDF or image, ready to print.

The PDF couldn't be prepared. Try downloading the image instead.

Names and photos never leave your browser: nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How it works

  1. Add your family. Use the “+” buttons to add siblings, aunts, uncles and cousins on each side, as many as you have.
  2. Type the names, or leave them blank to fill in by hand later.
  3. Add photos if you like with the camera button. If not, there's a round space to stick a photo or draw.
  4. Choose a style and download it as a PDF (A4 landscape) or as an image.

For a school project, the “Colorful”, “Space”, “Dinosaurs” and “Under the sea” styles work great, and “White” uses very little ink. Here are some ideas for doing it with kids: family tree for kids.
